# Style library

Starting points for new designs. Pick the one that fits the topic and audience (or blend two), adapt the palette to
the user's brand kit when they have one, and vary the choice between carousels so a user's feed doesn't look
templated. Font names must exist in the spec's font list; colors below are suggestions.

1. **Dark tech-editorial** — deep indigo/near-black base, electric cyan + warm amber duotone accents, bold Space
   Grotesk headings with Inter body. Icons per content slide, a highlighted "stat" pill, thin dividers, a low-opacity
   watermark number, a couple of rotated accent shapes. Confident, high-contrast. Good for: tools, tech, AI, dev.
2. **Warm minimal serif** — cream base (#F6F1E7), terracotta (#C4633B) + muted sage (#7C8B6F), Playfair Display
   headings with Inter body. No icons: thin line dividers, one small circle accent, big watermark numerals; lots of
   negative space. Calm, premium. Good for: habits, wellbeing, reflective or personal topics.
3. **Brutalist mono (works great square)** — near-black (#0B0B0B), off-white text, ONE loud accent (acid green
   #C6FF3D). Massive Space Grotesk / Archivo headings with tight line-height, hard-edged rectangles, thick 3–4px
   borders, rotated capsule "tag" pills. Confrontational, high-impact. Good for: hot takes, "stop doing X".
4. **Duotone data story** — light base (#F4F6FA), deep navy panels (#12203A), electric coral highlight (#FF5A5F),
   Manrope headings with Inter body. Big numbers, clean infographic structure. Good for: stats, research, reports.
5. **Vibrant playful** — each content slide on a different bold solid from a rotating set (coral #FF6B6B, teal
   #21C0A8, yellow #FFC93C, violet #7C5CFC), rounded cards and capsule pills, Poppins headings with Inter body.
   Energetic. Good for: money, lifestyle, beginner-friendly tips.
6. **Moody magazine quotes** — espresso/charcoal (#1E1A17), warm amber (#E0A34E), off-white text; dramatic serif
   (DM Serif Display / Playfair Display) pull-quote headings with small uppercase Inter captions. Good for: hard
   truths, lessons, opinion.
7. **Split-screen comparison** — every slide split into two halves with two large rectangles: cool slate (#2B3A55)
   and warm cream (#EFE7D8), Plus Jakarta Sans throughout. Structured, decisive. Good for: X vs Y, before/after.
8. **Instagram-native clean** — white base, Instagram accent palette (purple #833AB4 → magenta #C13584 → pink #E1306C
   → orange #F77737 → yellow #FCAF45) as dots, chips and thin accent bars (a row of solid circles reads as a gradient),
   Poppins headings with Inter body, ink #262626. Good for: Instagram growth, creator tips.
9. **Bold mobile / reels** — dark charcoal (#111114), hot-pink → orange accent, heavy Montserrat/Anton-style
   headings, a tall rounded-rectangle "phone/reel frame" with a play glyph (circle + triangle) and a thin progress
   line. Punchy, mobile-first. Good for: hooks, short-form video topics.
